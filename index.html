  1. DETECT

    Watch the feeds

    Monitor live video, alarm panels and access control events across client sites in real time.

    • AI video analytics alerts
    • Intrusion and motion zones
    • Forced and held-open doors
  2. VERIFY

    Confirm on camera

    Pull up live and recorded footage, cross-check badge logs, and decide in seconds if the event is real.

    • Multi-camera correlation
    • Badge-to-video matching
    • False-positive reduction
  3. RESPOND

    Dispatch and escalate

    Follow the site's SOP. Notify on-site guards, client contacts or police, and keep everyone updated until the event is resolved.

    • Dispatch coordination
    • Executive escalations
    • Shift handover notes
  4. INVESTIGATE

    Rebuild the timeline

    Trace the subject across cameras and access points to establish what happened, when, who was involved and why it was possible.

    • Footage review and tracking
    • Access log analysis
    • Root cause analysis
  5. DOCUMENT

    Preserve the evidence

    Export clips and logs under chain-of-custody standards and write an executive report the client can act on.

    • Chain of custody
    • Timestamped evidence
    • Executive incident reports
  6. PREVENT

    Close the gap

    Turn each finding into a fix: new camera angles, tighter intrusion zones, updated SOPs and loss prevention measures.

    • Camera coverage planning
    • Blind-spot removal
    • Playbook updates

Anatomy of my case file
CASE FILE · EXECUTIVE REPORT FORMATCHAIN OF CUSTODY ✓
Summary
What happened, where, and the impact, in three lines a client executive can read in 30 seconds.
Timeline
A minute-by-minute record built from camera timestamps, alarm events and badge reads.
Evidence
Exported clips, stills and access logs with a documented chain of custody.
Root cause
Why it was possible: a coverage gap, a process failure or a policy breach.
Risk exposure
What could have been lost, and what is still at risk if nothing changes.
Corrective actions
Specific fixes with owners: camera changes, intrusion zones, SOP updates.
